Emirates Gold

A Dubai refinery and mint with 30 years of history.

Emirates Gold refines, assays, smelts, trades and mints precious metals for local and international markets. Its bullion bars are refined up to 999.9 fineness, and it also makes custom bars and coins, separates precious stones from old jewellery and supplies corporate gifts.
