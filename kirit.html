Chapter IV · The Process

How gold moves through Emirates Gold.

Receiving

Every consignment of gold or silver is weighed, logged and documented the moment it arrives, then moved into secure storage. Clear paperwork at this first stage keeps each lot traceable from start to finish.

Assay

Samples from each lot are tested by fire assay and spectrometry. The results confirm purity and set the value before any pricing or processing begins.

Smelting and refining

Metal is melted and refined to the required fineness, up to 999.9 for bullion. Process checks and recorded yields keep the output accurate and consistent.

Casting and minting

Refined metal is cast into bars and granules, or minted into bars and coins, including custom designs. Every piece is weighed, marked and checked before it moves on.

Dispatch

Finished products are reconciled against the records, packed securely and released with complete documents. Logistics are coordinated so delivery to the client is on time and fully traceable.
